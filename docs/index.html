Abstract +

On-policy distillation (OPD) enables effective capability transfer between language models, yet the mechanisms underlying its failures are not fully understood. Across code generation and mathematical reasoning, OPD with larger-scale teachers exhibits early loss plateaus, with an average final loss reduction of 25.1% after 200 updates, compared with 96.2% for self-RL teachers, obtained by further reinforcement learning (RL) training of the initial student. To understand this difference, we analyze OPD as an idealized continuous-time dynamical system in the small-learning-rate limit. Our training-log diagnostics associate these plateaus with an early decline in a gradient-based learning-signal proxy while substantial loss remains; these measurements do not establish why the underlying gradient weakens. We further prove a local recovery guarantee for teachers sufficiently close to the initial student in a shared parameterization under regularity conditions, offering a conditional explanation for the success of self-RL teachers in our experiments. Across runs with and without loss plateaus, we observe small relative parameter changes (0.025–0.098%) and high similarity between the student’s representations before and after OPD (linear CKA >0.98 across layers). These observations suggest that limited representation adaptation may contribute to learning-signal collapse, a hypothesis that remains to be tested. Code is available on GitHub.

Nearby teachers. A local guarantee.

04

REPRESENTATION CHANGES

Representations barely move.

Linear CKA > 0.98 at all measured layers after 200 updates—in both successful and stalled runs.

A possible explanation. Limited adaptation may suffice for nearby self-RL teachers while leaving larger teacher–student differences unresolved. Its role in signal collapse remains untested.

05

ABLATIONS

Do training changes help?

Full logits, more rollouts, and an SFT warm start.

The teacher-dependent pattern persists.

Qwen3-4B-RL-Math outperforms Qwen3-4B in final accuracy and loss reduction across all three Qwen3-1.7B configurations.

Scope: one run per setting, 200 OPD updates. Conclusions apply to these recipes and budgets.
